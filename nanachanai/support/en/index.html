Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a figure photo?

  1. Tap "Generate" on the home screen
  2. Select a photo from your library
  3. Choose a preset or customize options
  4. Tap the "Generate" button

How do credits work?

Each generation costs 10 credits. Custom backgrounds and custom angles each add 5 additional credits.

What if my generation fails?

If generation fails, your credits will be automatically refunded. For best results, use clear, well-lit photos and ensure a stable internet connection.

How do I purchase credits?

Tap your credit balance in the top right, choose a credit package, and complete purchase via Apple Pay or credit card.

Tips for Best Results

Use clear, well-lit photos with higher resolution. Center your subject in frame and use portrait-oriented photos when possible. Single subjects work best.

Available Presets

Studio Light provides a clean white background. Cozy Desk offers a warm everyday setup. Gaming Setup features RGB lighting. Dark Showcase delivers an elegant display.

Privacy

The app requires photo library access to save generated figure photos and select photos for transformation. We never store or share your photos without permission.
