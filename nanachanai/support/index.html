❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a figure photo?

  1. Tap "Generate" on the home screen
  2. Select a photo from your library
  3. Choose a preset or customize options
  4. Tap the "Generate" button

How do credits work?

  • Each generation costs 10 credits
  • Custom backgrounds: +5 credits
  • Custom angles: +5 credits
  • Watermark removal is FREE! 🎉

What if my generation fails?

If generation fails, your credits will be automatically refunded. Try:

  • Using a clear, well-lit photo
  • Ensuring stable internet connection
  • Restarting the app if issues persist

How do I purchase credits?

  1. Tap your credit balance in the top right
  2. Choose a credit package that fits your needs
  3. Complete purchase via Apple Pay or credit card

Have other questions?

For any inquiries, please contact our support team at support@hexpy.games

💡 Tips for Best Results

📸 Photo Quality

  • Use clear, well-lit photos
  • Higher resolution = better results
  • Avoid blurry or dark images

🎯 Composition

  • Center your subject in frame
  • Use portrait-oriented photos
  • Single subject works best

🎨 Presets

  • Studio Light: Clean white background
  • Cozy Desk: Warm everyday setup
  • Gaming Setup: RGB lighting
  • Dark Showcase: Elegant display
