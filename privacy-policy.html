2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication credentials through Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In
  • User-Generated Content: Photos and images you upload for AI processing
  • Profile Information: Optional profile details you choose to provide
  • Purchase Information: Transaction records for in-app purchases (payment processing handled by App Store/Google Play)
  • Communications: Information from your correspondence with our support team

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information
  • Usage Data: App features used, generation history, interaction patterns, performance metrics
  • Log Data: IP address, app version, crash reports, system activity, timestamps
  • Analytics Data: App performance, user engagement metrics, feature usage statistics

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Providers: Basic profile information from Google or Apple when you sign in
  • Payment Processors: Transaction confirmations from App Store or Google Play
  • Advertising Partners: Ad interaction data from Google AdMob

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise Location Data: We do not collect GPS or precise location information
  • Biometric Data: We do not collect fingerprints, face recognition data, or other biometric identifiers
  • Health Information: We do not collect health or medical information
  • Contacts: We do not access your device contacts

  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Process in-app purchases and manage premium features

3.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features

  • Optimize AI generation quality and performance

  • Deliver personalized content and recommendations
